# Illustrative lunar surface assets

These are visual aids, not measurements of the selected lunar location.

- `moon-01-diffuse.jpg`, `moon-01-normal.jpg`: [Moon 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_01), a photogrammetry scan of lunar regolith simulant by Poly Haven, used under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The viewer desaturates the color and adjusts the shading.
- `moon-rock-02.glb`: [Moon Rock 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_02) by Poly Haven, used under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The geometry was reduced from 3,304 to 726 triangles for interactive use and is randomly placed as illustrative detail.
- `moon-rock-05.glb`: [Moon Rock 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_05) by Poly Haven, used under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The geometry was reduced from 9,850 to 689 triangles for interactive use.

Poly Haven describes the assets as scans of terrestrial lunar regolith simulant. They are not photographs or measurements from these landing sites. The scientific elevation mesh and coordinate picking remain separate.
