# Decorative lunar material assets

Source: Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com . CC0-1.0; redistribution and modification permitted.

Moon Rock 01 and Moon Rock 04: photographed laboratory rock models, not surveyed rocks at these eight landing sites. Moon Dusted 02: photographed regolith material, not measured landing-site reflectance. Artist and original download/checksum metadata are recorded in SOURCES.json.

The application retains the measured SLDEM/LOLA mesh for coordinates and elevation queries. These maps, stones and the optional shallow crater skin are illustrative. No claim of exact tutorial parity or photometric calibration is made.
